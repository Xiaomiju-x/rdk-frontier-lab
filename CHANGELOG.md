# Changelog

Notable public changes are recorded here. Versions describe the public tooling,
not the maturity or accuracy of every model in the research catalogue.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-09-03

Initial alpha public foundation:

- Dependency-free CLI for catalogue access, safe offline replay, fixture checks,
  receipt validation and three-target comparison.
- Explicit CPU/BPU/hybrid semantics, failure/incomplete separation and comparable
  timing boundaries; no automatic device access.
- First-party weighted Kabsch geometry port and MIT neural reference source.
- Metadata-only laboratory snapshot with upstream revision and asset-review notes.
- Sanitized results and SwinIR/RTMPose failure studies; no raw private evidence or models.
- English/Chinese entry points, schemas, tests, maintenance policies and CI.

Known limits: alpha APIs, incomplete full model recipe coverage, no PyPI release,
no proprietary runtimes or weights, and no public three-board benchmark certification.
